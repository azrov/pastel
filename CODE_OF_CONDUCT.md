# Code of conduct

Pastel follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to Pascal or to programming; criticize ideas, not people; no harassment of any kind. Many people here write in their second or third language, so read generously.

Problems can be reported privately to the maintainers through the contact on the repository's profile. Reports are handled confidentially.
