#Pastel.NativeViews

The phone's own views in a form: web pages, video, and sound.

Browser.Navigate('https://pastel.dev');
Browser.LoadHtml('<h1>Hello</h1><button onclick="pastel.post(''hi'')">Hi</button>');
Browser.EvaluateJavaScript('document.title', @TitleCame);
Video.Url := 'https://example.com/clip.mp4'; Video.Play;
Player.Url := '/sdcard/Music/song.mp3'; Player.Play;        // TMediaPlayer: no view

On Android TWebBrowser and TVideoView are Android's WebView and VideoView, over the app's window at the place of the control (they follow it as it moves, scrolls, hides; a menu or dialog over the form hides them while it is open). A web page talks to Pascal with pastel.post(text) (OnMessage). TMediaPlayer is Android's MediaPlayer; with [package.android] background = ["media_playback"] and StartBackgroundWork it plays on when the app is closed. On computers TWebBrowser shows the address and opens it in the browser on a click; TMediaPlayer plays with mpv, ffplay or VLC (Linux), afplay (macOS) or Windows Media Player; TVideoView opens the video in the video app.

uses Pastel.NativeViews; · source: framework/src/pastel.nativeviews.pas
