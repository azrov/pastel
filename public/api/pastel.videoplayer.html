#Pastel.VideoPlayer

TVideoPlayer: video and live streams in the form, on computers and phones.

Player.UserAgent := 'MyApp/1.0';                // some servers want one
Player.Play('http://example.com/live/42.m3u8');  // HLS, MPEG-TS, MP4, MKV...
Player.ShowInfo('Channel 1', 'Now: News 20:00-21:00');
Player.Seek(30, True);                           // 30 seconds on
for T in Player.Tracks do ...                    // audio and subtitles
Player.SelectTrack('audio', 2);

Computers: libmpv draws the picture into the window (Linux: the libmpv2 package; Windows: libmpv-2.dll next to the program). Android: ExoPlayer when the app has it ([package.android] libraries = ["androidx.media3:media3-exoplayer:1.4.1", "androidx.media3:media3-exoplayer-hls:1.4.1"]), else Android's MediaPlayer; the picture is the phone's own view over the window, with the info band and the spinner drawn by it.

Keys (the player has the focus): Enter/Space pause and play, Left/Right 10 seconds back and on (not live), M mutes; OnKeyDown sees them first (the arrows of a TV's remote: channels).
